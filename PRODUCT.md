# Product
## Register
product
## Users
Philippine drivers and small-business operators comparing regional fuel and utility reference prices.
## Product Purpose
Let a visitor see the best-supported available reference and its scope within 10 seconds; expose uncertainty and dated or missing evidence.
## Brand Personality
Clear, calm, responsive. October upgrade requests Apple-inspired translucent frosted controls and smooth interaction.
## Anti-references
Invented prices, residential rates represented as business tariffs, unreadable transparent text, decorative animation that delays work.
## Design Principles
Source-backed numbers first; map usability; legibility over effects; explicit units and applicability; interaction responds immediately.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion and reduced transparency fallback; price comparison uses numeric values as well as color.
